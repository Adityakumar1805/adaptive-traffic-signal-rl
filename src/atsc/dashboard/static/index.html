    What you are looking at

    Both grids get exactly the same vehicles at the same moments. On the left, four Double + Dueling DQN agents — one per junction — choose the next green phase every few seconds; on the right, a fixed-time plan cycles its phases on a timer. A safety state machine inserts the amber and all-red on both sides, and on both sides an emergency vehicle pre-empts the signal as soon as it reaches the stop line. Vehicle types are cosmetic: they change the picture, never the traffic.
